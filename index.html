How a CMMC engagement runs

  1. Scope

    Define where controlled unclassified information lives and which systems, people, and providers are in scope. A smaller, well-drawn boundary is the single biggest cost saver.

  2. Assess

    Test each of the 110 requirements the way an assessor would, and score where you stand today.

  3. Remediate

    Close gaps in priority order, with policies, procedures, and evidence written alongside the technical work, not after it.

  4. Prepare

    Rehearse the assessment, organize evidence, and confirm your system security plan matches reality before the assessors arrive.
