What we do

Governance, risk, and compliance

Risk assessments, policies, and compliance programs mapped to the frameworks you answer to, including the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, NIST SP 800-53, and NIST SP 800-171.

For defense contractors, end-to-end CMMC Level 2 preparation. For authorized assessment organizations (C3PAOs), certified assessors who join your assessment teams.

For organizations facing an assessment, an audit, or a customer's security requirements.

Incident response advisory

Incident response plans and playbooks, tabletop exercises, and post-incident reviews that make the next response faster.

When an incident happens, an experienced incident commander who convenes the right people fast, sets severity, coordinates third parties, and keeps executives, legal, privacy, and compliance informed, each in the form they need, until responsible closure.

For teams that want to be ready, and for those that need someone to lead the response now.

Security program building

A security program built from the ground up: a current-state assessment, a prioritized roadmap, policies and standards, and the processes, metrics, and governance that keep it running.

For organizations whose security has grown ad hoc, or never formally started.

Cybersecurity program leadership

Ongoing leadership of your cybersecurity program as a virtual CISO. We set priorities, report risk to executives and the board, manage the roadmap, and keep the program current as threats and requirements change.

For organizations that need a security leader but not a full-time hire.

How we work

  1. Assess

    Establish where you stand against the risks and requirements that matter to your business, tested the way an auditor or attacker would test them.

  2. Plan

    Turn findings into a prioritized roadmap tied to business risk, so the first dollars go where they reduce the most exposure.

  3. Build

    Put controls, processes, and documentation in place together, with evidence produced as the work is done, not reconstructed later.

  4. Sustain

    Measure, report, and improve, so the program is ready for the next assessment and the next incident.
